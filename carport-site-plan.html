How to draw a carport site plan

Short answer

A carport site plan is a scaled drawing of your block, usually at 1:200. It shows the boundaries, the house and other buildings, the carport, and the distance from its posts and roof to each boundary. Councils such as Wanneroo also want levels, any septic system and a north point.

Last updated 11 November 2026

What a carport site plan must show

The site plan is a bird's-eye drawing of your whole block. It's how the council checks your setbacks. For an uncertified (BA2) application, the City of Wanneroo asks for a site plan at a minimum scale of 1:200 showing:

  • Contour survey or spot levels, and the proposed finished floor level
  • How far the carport is from every lot boundary
  • How far it is from other buildings on the lot, including retaining walls
  • Any septic system
  • A north point

The City of Stirling's planning site plan also asks for a north point, the crossover and levels. Councils vary, so check your council's checklist.

Step by step

  1. Get your lot plan. Start with your Certificate of Title diagram, survey or the plans from when the house was built. Councils such as Stirling let owners request copies of approved plans.
  2. Draw the boundaries to scale. At 1:200, 1 cm on paper is 2 m on the ground. Label each boundary's length.
  3. Add what's already there. The house, sheds, pool, retaining walls, driveway and crossover.
  4. Draw the carport. Show its full outline and size. Mark the posts and the roof edge, because councils check setbacks to both.
  5. Dimension the setbacks. Measure from the posts and the roof edge to the street boundary, each side boundary, and the house.
  6. Add levels, north point, scale and address. Then date it.

What else to lodge

The site plan isn't enough on its own. Wanneroo also asks for floor plan, elevations and sections at 1:100, construction details and a structural engineer's details. Other councils usually ask for something similar.

Questions

Common questions

What scale should a carport site plan be?

At least 1:200. Wanneroo asks for a minimum of 1:200 for the site plan and 1:100 for elevations.

What does a carport site plan need to show?

Boundaries, existing buildings and retaining walls, the carport and its setbacks to boundaries and buildings, levels, any septic system and a north point. Stirling also asks for the crossover.

Can I draw my own carport site plan?

Usually yes, if it's to scale, dimensioned and shows everything on the council's checklist. Engineering must still come from a structural engineer.

Where do I get my lot dimensions?

From your Certificate of Title diagram, a survey, or the plans from when the house was built. Councils such as Stirling let owners request copies of approved plans.
