City of Belmont carport rules

Short answer

In Belmont, almost every carport needs a building permit. A carport in the front setback can skip planning approval only if it's open-sided, any door is see-through, the roof matches the house, and it's more than 2.0 m from the front boundary.

Last updated 16 November 2026

What counts as a carport

The City of Belmont defines a carport as a roofed structure for one or more cars that is open-sided (except where it meets the house or a boundary) and has no door, unless the door is visually permeable. Add a solid door and it's no longer a carport.

Building permit

Belmont needs a building permit for all carports, except one that is freestanding, under 2.4 m high and under 10 m². Since nearly every carport is bigger than 10 m², you should expect to need one.

Planning (development) approval

You need planning approval if the carport:

  • doesn't meet the standard R-Codes rules, or
  • doesn't meet the City's Local Planning Scheme No. 15, or
  • is in a heritage area, a Special Precinct Development lot, or within or next to the Swan Canning Development Control Area (generally properties near the river).

A carport in the front (primary street) setback also needs planning approval unless all four of these are true:

RequirementWhat it means
Open-sidedOnly closed where it meets the house and one side boundary
See-through doorAny door lets you see through to the house
Matching roofRoof pitch and colour match the house
More than 2.0 m backSet more than 2 m from the front boundary

If you need both approvals, the City won't issue the building permit until planning approval is granted and its conditions are dealt with.

What to lodge

  • A BA1 form with a BA3 certificate from a registered building surveyor, or a BA2 form.
  • For a BA2: a site plan (at least 1:200) with setbacks, levels and easements; elevations and a floor plan (at least 1:100); and structural design certified by a registered structural engineer.
  • The fees, and a CTF receipt if the work is over $100,000.

Lodge online through the City's Planning and Building portal, in person at 215 Wright Street, Cloverdale, or by mail.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a carport in the City of Belmont?

Yes. Belmont needs a building permit for all carports except freestanding ones under 2.4 m high and under 10 m².

How close to the front boundary can a carport be in Belmont?

Without planning approval, more than 2.0 m from the front boundary, if it's also open-sided, has a see-through door (if any) and a roof matching the house.

Can a Belmont carport have a door?

Only a visually permeable one. A solid door means it's not a carport under Belmont's definition.

How do I lodge a carport application in Belmont?

Online through the City's Planning and Building portal, in person at 215 Wright Street, Cloverdale, or by mail.
