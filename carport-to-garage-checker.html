Can I convert my carport to a garage?

Short answer

Adding a solid door turns a carport into a garage. Garages usually need to be 4.5 m from the front boundary or 3 m from a side street, and always need a building permit. Answer three questions below.

Carport to garage?

Up to three quick questions.

Why a door changes everything

Under the R-Codes, a carport and a garage are different things. Joondalup and Melville say a carport can have a door only if it's visually permeable. Put on a solid roller or panel door and it becomes a garage.

That matters because garages have to sit further from the street:

Stirling allows a garage closer than 4.5 m if it's at least 0.5 m behind the front of the house. Councils vary, so check your own.

You'll need a building permit

Joondalup says a building permit is always needed to build or convert a carport or garage. Your plans will need to show the door, any new walls and the setbacks.

Questions

Common questions

Does adding a roller door make a carport a garage?

Yes. Councils such as Joondalup and Melville say a carport can only have a visually permeable door. A solid roller door makes it a garage.

How far from the street does a garage need to be in WA?

Usually 4.5 m from the front boundary and 3 m from a side street. Some councils, such as Stirling, allow closer if the garage is at least 0.5 m behind the front of the house.

Do I need a permit to convert a carport to a garage?

Yes, a building permit. If the garage won't meet the setbacks, you'll probably need planning approval first.

Can I add a see-through door to my carport?

Usually, yes. A visually permeable door keeps it a carport, so carport setbacks still apply.
