Cost guide

Carport to garage conversion cost

Short answer

In Perth, a standard garage door is usually $3,000 to $5,000 installed, plus $770 to $990 for a motor. Add the cost of walls and a building permit. If the carport is closer than 4.5 m to the street, you may also need planning approval.

Why the rules can add cost

Adding a solid door turns a carport into a garage, and a garage has stricter rules:

  • Setback: under the R-Codes, garages usually sit at least 4.5 m from the front boundary. Carports can often be closer. If yours is closer than 4.5 m, you may need planning approval or a see-through door instead.
  • Frontage: garage doors usually can't take up more than 50% of the lot frontage.
  • Permit: you always need a building permit to convert a carport to a garage.

Since 1 July 2026, you don't need a registered builder for a garage or carport job under $50,000.

Questions

Common questions

How much does it cost to convert a carport to a garage in Perth?

A standard garage door alone is usually $3,000 to $5,000 installed, plus $770 to $990 for a motor. Walls and permit fees are extra, so get quotes.

Do I need a permit to turn my carport into a garage?

Yes. Converting a carport to a garage needs a building permit, and may need planning approval if it's closer than 4.5 m to the street.

Can I put a roller door on my carport?

A solid roller door makes it a garage, so garage rules apply. A see-through door can usually keep it a carport.

Do I need a registered builder to convert a carport?

Not if the work is under $50,000. That limit applies to garages and carports from 1 July 2026.
