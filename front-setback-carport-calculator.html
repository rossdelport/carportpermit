Front setback carport calculator

Short answer

A carport can come forward into the street setback by up to half (to 3 m from the boundary on R20 lots, 2 m on R30 and R40) if it's no wider than 60% of the frontage, open to views from the street and matches the house. Put your numbers in below.

The rules the calculator uses

A carport in front of the house is allowed into the street setback under the standard planning rules (the "deemed-to-comply" rules), as long as it meets all of these:

RuleLimit
Normal street setback6 m on an R20 lot; 4 m on an R30 or R40 lot
How far a carport can come forwardUp to half the setback: 3 m from the boundary on R20, 2 m on R30 or R40
WidthNo more than 60% of the lot frontage
ViewsAn unobstructed view between the house and the street (no solid door)
MaterialsCompatible with the house
EavesCan project up to 0.75 m into the setback

These numbers come from the City of Wanneroo's carport information sheet, which applies the R-Codes to most R20 and R30 lots. Other councils use the same R-Codes but can have local policies, and estates with a Local Development Plan can be different.

Worked example

A 15 m wide R20 lot. 60% of 15 m is 9 m, so a 6 m double carport is fine on width. The normal setback is 6 m, halved to 3 m. So the carport can come forward to 3 m from the front boundary, as long as it's open to the street and matches the house.

Other things to check

  • Side boundary. At Wanneroo, carports need 1 m from the side boundary to the post (1.5 m if longer than 14 m). Posts on the boundary are allowed if the carport is no longer than 10 m and no higher than 2.7 m, with the roof 500 mm back, but only behind the street setback.
  • Corner lots. 1.5 m from the secondary street.
  • Heritage. Heritage-listed properties and heritage areas need planning approval regardless.
  • Building permit. Every carport needs one, even when planning approval isn't needed.

Sources

  • Carports information sheet, July 2025 (City of Wanneroo)
  • Carports and garages information sheet (City of Stirling)

Questions

Common questions

How close to the front boundary can a carport be in WA?

Under the standard rules, up to half the normal street setback: 3 m from the boundary on an R20 lot (half of 6 m) or 2 m on an R30 or R40 lot (half of 4 m), if the carport meets the width, view and materials rules.

What is the 60% rule for carports?

A carport in the front setback can't be wider than 60% of the lot's frontage. On a 15 m wide lot, that's 9 m.

Can a carport have a garage door in the front setback?

Not under the standard rules. A carport must keep an unobstructed view between the house and the street, so a solid door means planning approval.

Does a front carport that meets the rules still need a permit?

Yes. Meeting the standard rules means you can skip planning approval, but every carport still needs a building permit.
