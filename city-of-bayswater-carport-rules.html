City of Bayswater carport rules

Short answer

In Bayswater, almost every carport needs a building permit. Since September 2026, a carport can sit 1.5 m from the front boundary if it's open on all sides, no wider than 60% of the frontage and no taller than 3.5 m. Character Protection Areas have stricter rules, including no roller doors in the street setback.

Last updated 17 November 2026

Building permit

Like other Perth councils, the City of Bayswater only exempts a freestanding structure that's no bigger than 10 m² and no taller than 2.4 m. Almost every carport is bigger, so expect to need a building permit. You can lodge a BA1 (certified, with a Certificate of Design Compliance from a private building surveyor) or a BA2 (uncertified). The City has 25 business days to decide a BA2.

Carports in the front setback

Bayswater's Residential Development Policy (adopted September 2026) changes the standard R-Codes rule. A carport can be as close as 1.5 m to the front boundary if:

RequirementLimit
WidthNo more than 60% of the lot frontage
SidesOpen on all sides
HeightNo more than 3.5 m

If your carport meets these and the other R-Codes rules, you usually won't need planning approval. If not, you'll need a development application first. The City runs a fast track service for simple applications such as carports.

Character Protection Areas

Parts of Maylands North, Mount Lawley and Bayswater are Character Protection Areas with stricter carport rules:

  • Parking must be at the rear, off the right of way, if there is one you can use. If not, from a side street, and only then from the front street.
  • A carport in the street setback must be open on all sides (except where it meets the house) and simple, so it doesn't hide the house.
  • Lots with a frontage of 12 m or less can only have a single-width carport.
  • Roofs should be flat or low-pitch skillion, lining up with the house verandah. Posts no wider than 90 × 90 mm.
  • No roller doors, tilt doors or solid gates on a carport in the street setback.
  • If there's already a garage, no carport is allowed in the front setback.

Heritage-listed places and heritage areas have their own rules, so check your lot on the City's online map.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a carport in the City of Bayswater?

Yes, almost always. Only freestanding structures up to 10 m² and 2.4 m high are exempt from a building permit.

How close to the front boundary can a carport be in Bayswater?

1.5 m, if it's open on all sides, no wider than 60% of the frontage and no taller than 3.5 m.

Can I put a roller door on a carport in a Bayswater Character Protection Area?

Not if the carport is in the street setback. Roller doors, tilt doors and solid gates aren't permitted there.

How long does Bayswater take to approve a carport?

Up to 25 business days for an uncertified BA2 building permit. Planning approval, if needed, comes first.
