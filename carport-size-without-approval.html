Question answer

What size carport can you build without approval?

Short answer

Effectively none. The exemption only covers freestanding structures up to 10 m² and 2.4 m high, and a car space is at least about 13 m². So every carport you can park under needs a building permit. Planning approval is different: most carports skip it if they stay within 60% of the frontage, keep views to the street and meet the setbacks.

Last updated 31 October 2026

Why there's no real "small carport" exemption

The WA Building Regulations exempt small freestanding structures from a building permit: 10 m² or less and no taller than 2.4 m. Some websites say a carport can use this. On paper it can, but look at the size of a car space:

SizeArea
Exemption limite.g. 3.2 m x 3.1 m10 m²
Minimum car space (City of Stirling)5.4 m x 2.4 mabout 13 m²

A real car won't fit under a 10 m² roof. That's why councils like Stirling and Wanneroo simply say a carport always needs a building permit. The exemption also only covers freestanding structures, and most carports are fixed to the house.

What size avoids planning approval?

This is the question that matters more. Planning approval isn't about a size limit; it's about whether the carport meets the standard rules for your lot. Most carports skip it if they:

  • Take up no more than 60% of the lot's frontage.
  • Keep a clear view between the house and the street (open sides, no solid doors).
  • Sit at least the reduced street setback. At Wanneroo, the normal setback (6 m at R20, 4 m at R30 to R40) can be halved for a carport that meets the frontage, open-sides and matching-materials rules.
  • Aren't on a heritage-listed property or in a heritage area.

Posts on a side boundary can also be allowed. Wanneroo accepts this for carports no longer than 10 m and no higher than 2.7 m, with the roof set 500 mm back. More on carports in the front setback.

So what should you do?

Plan on a building permit for any carport you can park a car under. For most, the council fees are about $183 in total. If yours is in the front setback or near the boundary, check the planning rules before you buy a kit, because that's where carports get refused.

Questions

Common questions

What size carport can you build without council approval in WA?

Effectively none. The building permit exemption only covers freestanding structures up to 10 m² and 2.4 m high, which is too small to park a car under. Councils such as Stirling and Wanneroo say every carport needs a building permit.

Does a carport need planning approval?

Not if it meets the standard planning rules: within 60% of the frontage, open to views from the street, at the right setback and not on a heritage property. Otherwise it needs planning approval first.

Can a carport be built on the boundary in WA?

Often, yes. The City of Wanneroo, for example, allows carport posts on the boundary if the carport is no longer than 10 m and no higher than 2.7 m, with the roof 500 mm back from the boundary.

What is the minimum size for a carport?

The City of Stirling's minimum car space is 5.4 m long and 2.4 m wide, about 13 m². That's already bigger than the 10 m² permit exemption.
