City of Armadale carport rules

Short answer

In the City of Armadale nearly every carport needs a building permit. Only freestanding ones under 10 m² and 2.4 m high are exempt. Setbacks follow the R-Codes and local policies, and you may need planning approval first. The City decides in 10 business days (BA1) or 25 (BA2).

Last updated 24 November 2026

Do you need a building permit?

Almost always. The City of Armadale only lets a carport skip the permit if it's freestanding, under 10 m² and no higher than 2.4 m. Any carport attached to the house or another structure needs a permit, whatever its size. Get the permit before any work starts.

Planning approval and setbacks

Setbacks come from the R-Codes and the City's local planning policies, so they depend on your lot. If your carport doesn't meet them, you may need planning approval first, before the building permit can be issued. Call the City on 9394 5000 if you're not sure.

Who can build it

If the work is worth $50,000 or more, you need a registered builder or owner-builder approval from the state. Most carports cost less than that.

Timeframes and after the build

  • BA1: up to 10 business days. BA2: up to 25 business days.
  • The permit lasts 2 years. You can apply for more time.
  • The builder must send a BA7 notice of completion within 7 days of finishing.

You can apply online on the City's website. See attached vs freestanding carports, carports in the front setback and carport site plans.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a carport in Armadale?

Yes, unless it's freestanding, under 10 m² and no higher than 2.4 m. All attached carports need a permit.

Does a carport need planning approval in Armadale?

Only if it doesn't meet the R-Codes or the City's planning policies. If it does need it, get planning approval before the building permit.

How long is an Armadale building permit valid?

Two years from the date it's granted. You can apply to extend it.

Can I build my own carport in Armadale?

Yes. You only need a registered builder or owner-builder approval if the work is worth $50,000 or more.
