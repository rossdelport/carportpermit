Question answer

Can you enclose a carport?

Short answer

Yes, but it usually becomes a garage. A carport can have a see-through door, but a solid door makes it a garage under the R-Codes. Garages usually need to be 4.5 m from the street instead of 3 m, so you may need planning approval. Expect a building permit either way.

Last updated 7 November 2026

A solid door turns a carport into a garage

Councils use the R-Codes definitions. A carport must stay unenclosed, but it can have a see-through (visually permeable) door, like an open-mesh or slatted gate. Add a solid door and it's classed as a garage. Both the City of Joondalup and the City of Melville say this on their websites.

That matters because garages have stricter rules, especially on how far back from the street they must be.

Carport vs garage setbacks

Typical front setbacks before planning approval is needed:

CarportGarage
Joondalup (R20)3 m from the street the house faces4.5 m, and 0.5 m behind the front of the house
Melville3 m from the primary street4.5 m from the primary street (3 m if the door is side-on and the street wall has windows)

So a carport sitting 3 m from the street is fine as a carport. Put a solid roller door on it and it may no longer meet the garage setback. That usually means you need planning approval, and it may not be granted.

Councils also limit how much of the frontage a garage can take up, usually 50% for single-storey homes.

Building permit

Expect to need one. Joondalup says a building permit is required for building or converting a garage or carport. Adding walls and a door changes how the structure handles wind, so engineering may be needed too.

Turning it into a room

Converting a carport into a bedroom, office or living space is a bigger job. In Joondalup, planning approval is needed if:

  • It's visible from the street and the materials don't match the house
  • A window or door to the new room is less than 1.5 m from a side or rear boundary
  • You'll no longer have the car parking the R-Codes need (usually two bays)

The building permit for a room has extra rules, including a 2.4 m minimum ceiling height, stopping moisture and termites coming through the old carport floor, hardwired smoke alarms for all bedrooms, and moving any meter box to an outside wall.

What to do

  1. Decide whether you want a see-through door (stays a carport) or a solid one (becomes a garage).
  2. Measure the setback from the street and compare it with your council's garage rule.
  3. If it doesn't meet the rule, talk to your council about planning approval before buying a door.
  4. Apply for the building permit.

Learn more in carport vs garage, check your setback with the front setback carport calculator, or see carport council approval.

Questions

Common questions

Can I put a roller door on my carport in WA?

You can, but a solid door makes it a garage under the R-Codes. Garages need a bigger street setback, usually 4.5 m, so you may need planning approval. A see-through door keeps it a carport.

Do I need approval to enclose a carport?

Usually, yes. Expect a building permit, and planning approval if the enclosed structure doesn't meet the garage rules, such as the street setback.

What's the difference between a carport and a garage in WA?

A carport is unenclosed, though it can have a see-through door. A garage is enclosed or has a solid door. Garages have stricter setback rules.

Can I convert my carport into a room?

Yes, with approvals. You'll need a building permit, and planning approval if it affects street appearance, boundary setbacks or required car parking. The room needs a 2.4 m ceiling and moisture protection.
