City of Wanneroo carport rules

Short answer

In Wanneroo a carport always needs a building permit. The City's sheet sets a 6 m street setback on R20 lots (4 m on R30 or R40), which can be halved if the carport is no wider than 60% of the frontage. Posts can go on the side boundary if it's no more than 10 m long and 2.7 m high.

Last updated 10 November 2026

Do I need approval?

The City of Wanneroo says a building permit is always required for a carport. You only need planning approval (a development application) as well if your carport doesn't meet the R-Codes, for example if it's too close to the street. If planning approval is needed, it must be granted before the building permit is issued.

A carport is a roofed structure for vehicles that stays open, except where it touches the house or a boundary on one side. A door is allowed only if you can see through it. Add a solid door and it's a garage.

Wanneroo's carport setbacks

These come from the City's carport information sheet (July 2025) and apply to most lots coded R20 or R30:

SetbackRule
Primary street6 m on an R20 lot, 4 m on R30 or R40. Can be halved if the carport is no wider than 60% of the frontage, keeps a clear view between house and street, and matches the house.
Secondary street (corner lots)1.5 m
Side boundary1 m to the post if the carport is 14 m long or less; 1.5 m if longer
On the boundaryPosts can sit on the boundary, with the roof, eaves and gutters at least 500 mm back, if the carport is no more than 10 m long and 2.7 m high and is behind the primary street setback
EavesCan project no more than 0.75 m into a setback

Rules can differ on other zonings or where a Local Development Plan or Structure Plan applies. The R-Codes were also updated in April 2026, so check your lot with the City before you design.

Building code distances

Separate from planning, the building code says a carport must be 900 mm from a side boundary or another building on the lot. This can drop to 500 mm if the carport has two or more open sides (at least a third of its perimeter open), a polycarbonate or non-combustible roof, non-combustible linings, and doesn't hold up any part of the house.

What to lodge

For an uncertified application (BA2), Wanneroo asks for:

  • A site plan at 1:200 with spot levels, finished floor level, setbacks to boundaries and buildings (including retaining walls), septic systems and a north point
  • Floor plan, elevations and sections at 1:100
  • Construction details and a structural engineer's details
  • A bushfire (BAL) report if needed
  • A Street and Verge Bond Permit application, which comes with every building permit

For a certified application (BA1), lodge the Certificate of Design Compliance and the documents it refers to. The City prefers online lodgement. Already built without a permit? Wanneroo has a separate checklist for unauthorised works.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a carport in Wanneroo?

Yes. The City of Wanneroo says a building permit is always required for a carport. Planning approval is only needed as well if the carport doesn't meet the R-Codes.

How close to the street can a carport be in Wanneroo?

The City's July 2025 sheet says 6 m on an R20 lot and 4 m on R30 or R40, halved if the carport is no wider than 60% of the frontage, keeps a view to the street and matches the house.

Can I build a carport on the boundary in Wanneroo?

Yes, the posts can sit on the boundary if the carport is no more than 10 m long and 2.7 m high, behind the street setback, with the roof, eaves and gutters at least 500 mm back.

Do I need a verge bond for a carport in Wanneroo?

You need to apply for a Street and Verge Bond Permit with every building permit. It has a non-refundable admin fee, and a bond depending on the value of the work.
