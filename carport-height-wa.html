Question

How high can a carport be in WA?

Short answer

There's no single limit. On the boundary of most house lots, a carport can be up to 2.7 m high and 10 m long without planning approval. On denser R50+ lots, up to 3 m wall height and 4.2 m ridge. A carport always needs a building permit, whatever its height.

Last updated 14 November 2026

There's no single height limit

WA doesn't set one maximum height for every carport. Height matters in three places: on the boundary, in the front setback, and for the building permit exemption.

On or near the boundary

Lot typeCarport can go on the boundary without planning approval if
Most houses (R-Codes Part B: R40 and below)No more than 2.7 m high and 10 m long, behind the front setback, roof edge at least 450 mm from the boundary
Denser lots (R-Codes Part C: R50 and above)Less than 10 m long, wall height up to 3 m, ridge up to 4.2 m

The City of Wanneroo's carport sheet uses the same 2.7 m and 10 m limits, with the roof 500 mm back. If your carport is taller, it needs to be set back from the boundary, or you need planning approval.

Gable roofs add height

A gable or pitched roof is taller at the peak than a flat roof. If you're building near the boundary, check the whole carport, not just the posts, against the limits above. See flat vs gable carports.

Height and the building permit

Don't rely on the small-structure exemption. Councils such as Wanneroo and Stirling say a carport always needs a building permit, whatever its height.

In the front setback

Councils focus on how a carport in front of the house looks. The City of Melville, for example, wants a carport in the front setback to match the house's materials, style and roof pitch, and leave a clear view to the street. Councils vary, so check yours.

Questions

Common questions

How high can a carport be in WA?

There's no single limit. On the boundary of most house lots it must be no more than 2.7 m high and 10 m long to avoid planning approval. Away from the boundary, height is less restricted.

Can a carport on the boundary be higher than 2.7 m?

On most house lots, not without planning approval. On R50 and denser lots, the R-Codes allow up to 3 m wall height and 4.2 m ridge.

Does a low carport avoid a building permit?

No. Councils such as Wanneroo and Stirling say a carport always needs a building permit.

Does a gable carport count the peak height?

Yes, the whole structure counts, so a gable roof can push a boundary carport over the limit.
