Cost guide

Carport prices in Perth (2026), approvals included

Short answer

Perth businesses advertise $5,500 to $8,500 for an installed single carport and $7,000 to $12,500+ for an installed double. DIY kits start around $2,000. On top of that, government permit fees for a $10,000 carport are about $182.65, plus planning approval if your council needs it.

Last updated 6 November 2026

What pushes the price up

  • Size and height: a double (about 6 m x 6 m) costs more than a single (about 3 m x 6 m). Extra height for a 4WD or caravan adds more.
  • Roof style: flat is cheapest; gable costs more.
  • Ground: sloping or uneven ground, or a new slab, adds to the bill.
  • Extras: drainage, lighting or an EV charger (licensed electrician needed).

The approval costs

Most carports in WA need a building permit. The government fees are small next to the build:

FeeAmountOn a $10,000 carport
Building permit (BA2, uncertified)0.32% of the value, at least $121$121
Building Services Levy$61.65 for work up to $45,000$61.65
Total government fees$182.65

A carport in the front setback, or one that doesn't meet the R-Codes, may also need planning approval, which has its own fee. You'll also usually need engineering for the structure, which kit suppliers often include.

See the full breakdown in carport approval cost in Perth, or check whether yours needs planning approval with the front setback carport calculator.

Comparing quotes

Ask each supplier what's included: engineering, the permit application, footings or slab, drainage and the wind rating for your area. A cheap kit quote may leave the approval and site work to you.

Questions

Common questions

How much does a carport cost in Perth?

Perth businesses advertise about $5,500 to $8,500 for an installed single carport and $7,000 to $12,500+ for an installed double. DIY kits start around $2,000 to $5,000 for a single.

How much is a double carport kit in Perth?

Advertised prices range from about $4,000 to $7,500 for materials only, depending on the supplier, roof style and wind rating.

How much does carport council approval cost in Perth?

Government fees for a $10,000 carport are about $182.65: a $121 minimum BA2 permit fee plus the $61.65 levy. Planning approval, if needed, is extra.

Do I need council approval for a carport in WA?

Usually, yes. Most carports need a building permit, and some also need planning approval, for example in the front setback.
