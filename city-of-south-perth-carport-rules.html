City of South Perth carport rules

Short answer

In the City of South Perth, a carport needs a building permit. It can come forward by up to half the usual street setback if it's 0.5 m from the side boundaries, no wider than 60% of the frontage, has no walls in the setback, keeps a clear view and has no storeroom.

Last updated 28 November 2026

Building permit

The City of South Perth lists carports among the structures that need a building permit. Lodge a BA1 (certified by a private building surveyor) or a BA2 (checked by the City).

Street setback

South Perth's Local Planning Policy 1.1 (Residential Development, last changed August 2025) changes some R-Codes rules. A carport normally follows the usual primary street setback for your zoning. It can come forward by up to half that setback if:

  • the carport and its posts are at least 0.5 m from the side boundaries,
  • it's no wider than 60% of the lot frontage,
  • it has no walls in the part that sits inside the street setback,
  • you can see clearly between the house and the street, and
  • it has no enclosed storeroom.

The 0.5 m side gap and the storeroom rule are extras South Perth adds on top of the R-Codes. Work out your numbers with the front setback carport calculator.

Garages are set back further

For most houses (R-Codes Part B), the policy sets garages 5.0 m from the primary street, instead of the usual 4.5 m. A garage joined to the house can be closer if it sits at least 0.5 m behind the front of the house. See carport vs garage.

Side and rear boundaries

Under the R-Codes, a carport can usually have its posts on the boundary if it's no more than 10 m long and 2.7 m high, sits behind the street setback, and its roof, eaves and gutters are at least 450 mm from the boundary. The building code has its own fire rules too. See carports on the boundary.

If you can't meet these rules

A carport that doesn't meet the policy and the R-Codes needs planning (development) approval before the building permit. Call the City on 9474 0777.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a carport in South Perth?

Yes. The City of South Perth lists carports as needing a building permit.

How close to the street can a carport be in South Perth?

Up to half the usual primary street setback, if it's 0.5 m from side boundaries, no wider than 60% of the frontage, has no walls in the setback, keeps a clear view and has no enclosed storeroom.

Can a South Perth carport have a storeroom?

Not if you want the reduced street setback. The policy says the carport must not include an enclosed storeroom.

How far back must a garage be in South Perth?

Usually 5.0 m from the primary street for most houses, or closer if the garage joins the house and sits 0.5 m behind its front.
