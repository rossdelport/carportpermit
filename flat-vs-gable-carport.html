Flat vs gable carport

Short answer

A flat carport is cheaper and lower. A gable usually costs 20 to 30% more but can match a pitched-roof house. Both need a building permit. The main approval difference is height: on the boundary, a carport must usually be no more than 2.7 m high.

Cost

Phoenix Patios advertised installed double carports from about $7,000 to $12,500+ in January 2026, and says a gable roof costs 20 to 30% more. Prices vary by size, wind rating and site.

Approval: the roof shape doesn't change the need for a permit

Both styles need a building permit. The City of Wanneroo and the City of Stirling both say a carport always needs one.

Where roof shape matters is height. Under the R-Codes, a carport's posts can sit on the boundary if it's no more than 10 m long and 2.7 m high, sits behind the front setback, and the roof edge is at least 450 mm from the boundary (Wanneroo's sheet says 500 mm). A gable's peak adds height, so check your design against these limits.

Matching the house

For carports in the front setback, councils look at whether the carport suits the house. Wanneroo's reduced setback needs compatible materials, and Stirling says materials should match. A gable often blends in better with a pitched-roof house. Councils vary, so check yours.

Which should you pick?

  • Flat if you want the lowest price, a modern look, or need to stay under a height limit on the boundary.
  • Gable if you want it to match a pitched-roof house and don't mind paying more.

Questions

Common questions

Is a gable carport more expensive than a flat one?

Usually yes. Phoenix Patios says a gable roof adds 20 to 30% to the price.

Does a gable carport need council approval?

Yes. Both flat and gable carports need a building permit. Councils such as Wanneroo and Stirling say a carport always does.

Can a gable carport go on the boundary?

Only if it meets the limits: under the R-Codes, posts on the boundary are allowed if the carport is no more than 10 m long and 2.7 m high and behind the front setback. Check the peak height.

Which carport roof looks best?

A gable often matches a pitched-roof house. A flat roof suits a modern house. Councils like the carport to suit the house.
