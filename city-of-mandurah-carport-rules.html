City of Mandurah carport rules

Short answer

In the City of Mandurah, every carport needs a building permit. Planning approval usually isn't needed if it meets the R-Codes deemed-to-comply rules. If it doesn't, you need development approval first. The City offers a paid deemed-to-comply check to confirm.

Last updated 25 November 2026

Do you need a building permit?

Yes. The City of Mandurah says a building permit is always required for a carport. It treats a carport as a structure with a waterproof roof, open on two or more sides, with at least a third of its outside edge open.

Planning approval

Usually not needed if the carport meets the R-Codes deemed-to-comply rules. Whether you're exempt depends on your lot's zoning, site cover, setbacks and any special conditions. The City offers a deemed-to-comply check (a fee applies) to confirm in writing before you start.

Setbacks come from:

  • the City's town planning scheme,
  • the R-Codes,
  • local planning policies, and
  • any detailed area plan for your estate.

You also need to keep the minimum open space and outdoor living area. If the carport breaks any of these, you need development approval first. The building permit can't be decided until that's approved. Check your zoning on the City's online map.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a carport in Mandurah?

Yes. The City of Mandurah says a building permit is always required for a carport.

Does a carport need planning approval in Mandurah?

Usually not, if it meets the R-Codes deemed-to-comply rules. If it varies them, you need development approval first.

How close to the boundary can a carport be in Mandurah?

It depends on your zoning, the R-Codes, local policies and any detailed area plan. The City's deemed-to-comply check can confirm it for your lot.

How do I lodge a carport application in Mandurah?

Online, through the Building Lodgements link on the City's website. Paper applications are also accepted.
